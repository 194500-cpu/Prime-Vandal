This project is an aethestic mod to change the Nerf Retaliator into a functional Valorant Prime Vandal replica!
The final product that I created is seen above in the banner.
<br>
<br>
**Features:**
-Moving Bolt that loads standard size foam darts.
-Magazine that holds darts and is removable
-Non-permanent and beginner friendly design
-Completely opensourced files
-Printable even on an A1 mini (180mm x 180mm x 180mm 3d-printer)
-Fun!
<br>
<br>
All that is needed to start is a 3d printer and one roll of PLA!
<br>
<br>
**Hints**
-If the parts are too tight and won't fit, I highly recommend using a hair dryer. It has saved me so much time in this build. Simply heat up the part that is too tight, then quickly press it into place. The heated PLA should be soft and fit.
-The dowels are there to support the parts, not hold them. Use hot glue or superglue to hold two parts together once the dowels are put in. I HIGHLY recommend hot glue as you can easily take the parts apart if something goes wrong.



**BUILD INSTRUCTIONS**
1) Disassemble the Nerf Retaliator. If you are unsure on hwo to do this, check out this video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=felUcKv64l4<br>
2) Once you have taken all the components out of the retaliator, locate a metal screw holding the priming slide in place. Unscrew it and take off the slide. <br>
<img width="870" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7a44d06f-78f9-459c-9592-2841b67eb409" /><br>
3) Take the bolt (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/bolt.stl) and screw it into one of the slots where the slide was held in place with the same screw that held the slide.<br>
4) Re-assemble the retaliator. Check out the video in step 1 if unsure.<br>
5) Connect Muzzle 1 (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/muzzle1.stl) to Muzzle 2 (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/muzzle2.stl) using the muzzle dowel (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/muzzledowel.stl). You will need to print 3 muzzle dowels.<br>
6) Connect Stock 1 (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/stock1.stl) to Stock 2 (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/stock2.stl) using the stock dowel (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/stockdowel.stl). You will need to print 5 stock dowels.<br>
7) Connect the stock to the stock extender (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/stockextender.stl) by simply press fitting and gluing the two pieces together. <br>
8) Connect Top 1 (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/top1.stl) to Top 2 (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/top2.stl) using the top dowel (https://github.com/194500-cpu/Prime-Vandal/blob/main/STL-Files/topdowel.stl). You will need 2 top dowels.<br>
9) Paint the parts if you wish!<br>
10) Screw in the muzzle by simply using the retaliator's attachment point. Look at the hair dryer method in the hints section above if it is too tight! YOU DO NOT NEED GLUE<br>
11) Push the stock into the retaliator stock attachment point by press fitting it. YOU DO NOT NEED GLUE<br>
12) Glue the top onto the top of the Retaliator.<br>
13) ENJOY YOUR PRIME VANDAL!<br>
